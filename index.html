GridShift

Slide. Match. Survive.

A cozy puzzle game for iPhone. Slide rows and columns of cute blocks to line up three of a kind – before the board fills up.

Coming soon to the App Store

  • Simple to learn, hard to master
  • Two boards: 4×4 and 5×5
  • Chain matches to build huge combos
  • Day and night mode, and color symbols for colorblind players
  • No account needed – works offline
