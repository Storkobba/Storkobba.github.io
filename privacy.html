GridShift

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026

In short: GridShift does not ask for your name, email or any account. Your scores and settings stay on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some data from your device to show and measure those ads. In Europe you decide whether ads may be personalized.

1. Who we are

GridShift is developed by Jonas Birkedal, an individual developer based in Norway ("we", "us"). For questions about privacy, contact gridshiftsupport@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves your best scores, your settings (sound, vibration, dark mode, color symbols) and your current game on your device. This data is never sent to us. It is deleted when you delete the app.

3. Data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal data ourselves. GridShift has no user accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of our own.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

GridShift uses Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC, to show ads. When an ad is requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device and advertising identifiers, including Apple's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information, such as model, operating system and language
  • Information about ads you see and tap, and performance and crash data for the ad service

This data is used to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and – if you agree – show ads based on your interests. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. Read more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Your choices

Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

The first time you open the game, you see a consent message from Google. You can accept, decline or choose which purposes and ad partners you allow. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy settings.

Tracking on iPhone

Apple asks whether GridShift may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared. You can change this at any time in the iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

6. Legal basis

For users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, personalized advertising and storing or accessing information on your device for advertising are based on your consent. Some ad partners may rely on legitimate interest for certain purposes, such as measuring ads or preventing fraud. These are shown in the consent message, where you can object.

7. Children

GridShift is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. How long data is kept

Data stored on your device is kept until you delete the app. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw your consent. Since we do not hold personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are best handled through your choices in section 5 or through Google. You can also contact us, and you have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Norway this is Datatilsynet.

10. Changes

We may update this policy, for example if the game gets new features. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
