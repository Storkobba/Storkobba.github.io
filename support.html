Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Drag a row sideways or a column up or down. When three or more blocks of the same color line up, they disappear. New blocks arrive after every move – the rings show where they will land. The game ends when the board is full.

How do combos work?

Make a match on several moves in a row to build a combo. Each combo step multiplies the points for that match.

My best score is gone. Can I get it back?

Scores and settings are stored only on your device. If you delete the app, they are deleted too, and we cannot restore them.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep GridShift free. You can watch a short ad to get a second chance when the board is full, and a short ad may appear between some rounds.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, open Settings → Privacy settings in the game. To change whether apps may track you, open the iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The game does not start or crashes

Make sure your iPhone is updated to the latest iOS version, then restart the phone. If the problem continues, email us and tell us what happened.
